GeoSort Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Document version: 2026-11-06-1

Public copy: https://randclanenterprises.github.io/geosort-legal/privacy/

1. The short version

GeoSort runs on your phone. It has no accounts and no GeoSort server. We do not collect, receive or sell your photos, your location or any other personal information. Everything GeoSort remembers stays on your device.

Two Google services are built in: Google Maps, which draws the map (section 5), and Google AdMob, which shows the optional "Watch an ad" video in the free version (section 6).

RandClan Enterprises ("we" or "us") makes GeoSort. Questions about privacy: randclanenterprisesllc@gmail.com

2. What GeoSort does

GeoSort sorts your photos and videos into folders based on where they were taken. You draw circles on a map, called bubbles, and link each one to a folder. When you tap Sort, GeoSort reads the GPS location saved in each photo or video and moves it into the folder of the bubble it falls in. It does not look at what is in your pictures.

The optional Screenshots tab lists your screenshots grouped by the app they came from. GeoSort works out the app from the screenshot's file name. You can set rules for each app, view screenshots, and send screenshots to the trash. The tab does not move or rename any files.

3. What GeoSort can access on your device

Photos and videos. With your permission, GeoSort reads your photo and video library: file names, folders, dates, sizes, and the GPS location stored in each file. It needs this to sort them. If you turn on the Screenshots tab, it also shows thumbnails and full-size views of your screenshots on screen. This all happens on your device.

Your location. If you allow it, GeoSort uses your phone's location while the app is open so the map can show where you are and so you can place a bubble at your current spot. This is separate from the GPS saved inside your photos. GeoSort never asks for background location and never tracks you.

Installed apps (optional). If you choose "Show installed app icons", GeoSort looks up the names and icons of apps on your phone that have a launcher icon. It uses them only to show the right icon next to each app in the Screenshots tab. This stays on your device. You can turn it off anytime in Settings, then Legal and Privacy. The Screenshots tab still works without it, with plain icons.

Things GeoSort does not access: your contacts, messages, microphone, camera, or passwords.

4. What GeoSort saves on your device

GeoSort saves your bubbles (names, places, sizes), your folder links, your settings, your Screenshots rules, your sort tokens, the map position you last viewed, and a record that you accepted the terms. This is stored in the app's private storage and is not sent anywhere. GeoSort asks Android not to include this data in cloud backups.

Files you sort are moved to folders under DCIM/GeoSort on your device. They stay on your device.

Local Backup (Pro, optional) saves a plain text file with your bubbles, folder links and settings to DCIM/GeoSort/_Backups. It does not contain your photos or videos. The file is not encrypted and may show where your bubbles are. Other apps that can read that folder can read the file. Treat it like any personal file.

5. Google Maps

The map is provided by Google Maps. When a map is on screen, your phone talks to Google to load it. Google may receive your IP address, device and app information, and the area being viewed. Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to that:

Google Maps terms: https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

GeoSort does not send your photos, your photo locations or your list of installed apps to Google.

When you open a link from this app, your browser opens it. The site you visit sees what any website sees, such as your IP address.

6. Ads and purchases

In the free version, when you run out of sort tokens you can choose to watch a short ad to refill them. These ads come from Google AdMob. GeoSort has no analytics of its own.

To show and measure ads, Google's ad software in the app collects some information from your phone and sends it to Google. This includes your device's advertising ID, your IP address, the type of phone and Android version, app and ad interaction data (such as which ads were shown or tapped), and diagnostic data such as crashes. Google may use this to show ads that suit you, to limit how often you see the same ad, to count views, and to prevent fraud. GeoSort never gives Google your photos, your photo locations, your bubbles or your list of installed apps.

If you live in a region that requires it, such as the EU, UK or Switzerland, GeoSort asks for your consent before ads are loaded, using Google's consent screen. You can change your answer anytime in Settings, then Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's Android settings under Privacy, then Ads.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Pro is bought through Google Play, which handles payment under its own policies. We never see your card or payment details. Pro users are not shown the ad option.

7. Sharing

We do not receive your data, so we have nothing to sell or share. The ad data described in section 6 goes from your phone straight to Google, not to us. If you email us, we get your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and fix problems, and we keep it only as long as we need to. Please do not send photos or exact locations unless they are needed to solve a problem.

8. Deleting your data

You are in control of everything GeoSort stores.

To remove GeoSort's saved settings, bubbles and rules, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

Uninstalling does not move your sorted photos back and does not delete files in DCIM/GeoSort, including backup files. Delete those yourself with your gallery or a file manager. To undo a sort, use the return options in the app before you uninstall.

Because we hold none of your data, we cannot delete anything on our side. Anything shared with Google, including for ads, is covered by Google's policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to see, correct, delete or move your personal data, or to object to how it is used. We do not hold personal data about you, other than any email you send us. Write to randclanenterprisesllc@gmail.com about any of this and we will reply within the time the law requires.

10. Children

GeoSort is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has sent us information, write to randclanenterprisesllc@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Security

Your data stays on your phone, protected by Android's own app protections. GeoSort does not add its own encryption to backup files. Keep your phone updated and be careful which apps you let into your photos and folders.

12. Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date and version above and show the new version in the app. If a change affects how your data is used, we will ask you again.
