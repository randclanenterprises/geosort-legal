4. What GeoSort does

GeoSort moves your photos and videos into folders based on the GPS location saved in each file and the bubbles you draw. The Screenshots tab groups screenshots by app name taken from the file name, and can send screenshots to the trash after Android asks you to confirm.

Free lets you create up to 4 bubbles and uses sort tokens. A sort that moves at least one file uses 1 token. You can refill your tokens when you run out by watching a short rewarded ad from Google AdMob. The refill is given only after the ad finishes, and an ad may not always be available. Existing bubbles above the Free limit are not deleted. Pro currently adds unlimited bubbles, video sorting, the Screenshots tab, Local Backup, no ads and no sort token limit. Pro also lets you turn off New folder on a bubble, so photos taken there stay in DCIM/Camera and are shown in GeoSort's own gallery for that bubble instead of being moved. Features and limits can change in future versions.

GeoSort cannot promise that every file will be found or sorted correctly. GPS data in a file can be missing or wrong, and a file name may not match the app that made the screenshot.

5. Moving and deleting files

When you press Sort or choose to return files, you allow GeoSort to move files between folders on your device. GeoSort is built not to overwrite or delete your photos and videos, but moving files changes where they live, and other apps and galleries may show them in new places. Renaming a folder changes the album name too.

If you turn off New folder on a bubble, GeoSort can, if you choose, move the photos and videos in that bubble's folder back to DCIM/Camera and remove the folder once it is empty. The bubble stays on the map.

If an operation is interrupted, some files may have moved and others not. Check the folders if that happens.

Screenshots you send to the trash go to Android's trash and can be restored from your gallery or Files app for a limited time. After that Android removes them for good.

Keep your own backup of photos you cannot replace. We are not responsible for files you lose through your own actions, device problems, or third-party software.

6. Local Backup

Local Backup saves your bubbles, folder links and settings. It does not save your photos, videos, screenshots, tokens or purchases. The backup is a plain file in DCIM/GeoSort/_Backups and is not encrypted. Restoring a backup replaces your current bubbles and settings with the saved ones. Backups can be missing or damaged, so do not rely on them as your only copy.
